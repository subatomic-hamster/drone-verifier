Conventions. World frame: x east, y north, z up, origin at the pad; z = 0 is the standing height of the centre of mass. Body frame: x forward, y left, z up (along the thrust), aligned with the world on the pad. `s.att` is body to world.

The vanes. Vane k = 0, 1, 2, 3 sits at +x, +y, -x, -y in the exhaust, below the centre of mass. `vanes` are servo goal angles in radians, limited to about +-17 degrees by what stays effective. A positive angle pushes vane 0 toward +y, vane 1 toward -x, vane 2 toward -y, vane 3 toward +x; the force grows with the fan's thrust, `cfg.vanes.force_per_rad_per_newton` N per rad per N of thrust at small angles. Torque is lever arm cross force, so forces along the same tangential direction yaw the vehicle and opposed ones roll or pitch it. The servos lag the goal by tens of milliseconds and have gear backlash.

The fan. `duty` is 0 to 1. It takes about a second to spool up. `cfg.fan.thrust_motor_volts` is a table of thrust (N) against motor volts in still air, so duty is about volts / s.vbat; the pack sags under load. The fan spins about +z: its angular momentum (`spin_inertia` x speed, about 0.1 N m s in a hover) is comparable with the body's inertia times its rates, and the stator leaves `yaw_torque_per_newton` x thrust of reaction torque on the airframe.

`cfg` fields: g, dt, t_cal, mass, cg (m, deck frame, origin in the deck's top face on the fan axis), inertia (Ixx, Iyy, Izz about the centre of mass), h_stand, thrust_max_n, mission.{alt_m, alt_tol_m, hold_s, land_radius_m, touchdown_m_s, tilt_max_deg, mission_s}, fan.{thrust_motor_volts, spin_inertia, ct, cq, diameter, rho, yaw_torque_per_newton, current_limit_a}, vanes.{site_rel (4 x 3, m from the centre of mass), force_dir (4 x 3, per positive angle), force_per_rad_per_newton, area, exit_area, limit_rad}, battery.{cells, capacity_ah, r_internal_ohm}, range_sensor.{min_m, max_m}. `design_report` shows them for your design.

`s` fields: pos, vel, att, rate (numpy arrays); height (range sensor height of the centre of mass above ground, or None); vbat; armed; imu.gyro, imu.accel; baro (Pa, or None when there is no new sample); gnss (lat, lon, alt, vn, ve, vd, or None when there is no new fix). The estimate settles after calibration: pos starts with a GNSS error of your receiver's size that shrinks over seconds, and the position error is what limits your landing accuracy. While `s.armed` is false the command is ignored.

The flight clock `t` starts at 0 on the pad, and the mission limit counts from there. The vehicle must leave the ground by t = 15 s. It has landed when its feet stay on the ground; the flight ends when it has been at rest for 1 s, so cut the fan after touchdown. A step that raises an exception or returns NaN ends the controller: the fan stops.
